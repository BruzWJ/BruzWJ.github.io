After pointing Preferences at the two NVIDIA model repositories, Selected WAV playback or live PCM starts inference automatically and drives matching Shape Keys as frames arrive.

<ProjectSection
  eyebrow="Emotion mixing"
  title="Preferred emotion and auto emotion mixing"
  summary="Saved Preferred Emotion sliders can drive the face directly, or mix into Audio2Emotion output. Mixed Emotion is the worker's read-only effective tensor."
>
  <ProjectSectionCopy>
    Preferred Emotion is an authored, saved source. Load/Clear includes or removes it from the mix without copying values either way. Auto Audio2Emotion stays independent, so you can keep a preferred character while the generated mix still follows the audio. Control edits replace the active stream settings in place; playback, the worker, and loaded models stay up.
  </ProjectSectionCopy>

  title="Package-local GPU worker, Shape Key delivery"

  reverse

  <ProjectSectionCopy>
    Start Worker launches the child process and loads both models. Play on a selected WAV, or the first live PCM chunk, is what starts inference. Each delivered frame writes exact model channel names onto Mesh, Curve, Surface, and Lattice Shape Keys; missing names are skipped, and the Blender mesh does not need Audio2Face topology.
